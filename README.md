# liga_five_guys

Advice for a five-manager LaLiga Fantasy league: who to field, who to buy,
and who to sell to pay for them. Personal use; don't
redistribute the scraped data.

## Where it runs

On the Asus box under systemd. `tools/lfg-run` (linked from
`~/.local/bin/lfg-run`) runs the self-tests and the fetch, then `src/run.py`,
then commits. Every run, whoever asks for it (timer, report button),
runs every stage of `src/run.py` in one process; the report is
published to the site (notes.lemonworlds.com/fantasy) as soon as it is made. The page's "Run again" button leaves
a request (`rerun.json` in `$LIST_DATA_DIR/fantasy`) that `lfg-watch.timer` picks up within a minute.

    systemctl --user list-timers | grep lfg     # lfg, lfg-flip, lfg-watch, lfg-gc
    systemctl --user start lfg.service          # run now
    journalctl --user -u lfg.service -n 50      # what happened

`lfg.service` runs with MemoryMax=750M and a 10-minute timeout. The timers
run the working tree, so keep it green: work in a worktree, check
`systemctl --user is-active lfg.service` before merging. Give a worktree its
own `.venv` (`uv sync` in it), never a link to this one: `uv run` installs
the project it runs in into the venv it uses, so a linked venv runs the
timers on the worktree's code.

## What you edit

`inputs/league.ini` (who you are and the budget). Every ~90
days, or when the report says so: `python -m ffcore.auth --login`.
Everything else comes from the app's API and public pages.

## The pipeline (`src/run.py`)

1. **parse** (`sources.py`, `ingest.py`): every fetched page is archived in
   `data/raw/`; parsers turn it into the parse cache (`data/tidy/parsed.jsonl`, `snapindex.json`). `sources.py` says
   what to fetch; the parsers live with their provider (`ffcore/futbolfantasy`,
   `ffcore/footballdata`, `ffcore/laliga_api`). Each cached page is keyed by a
   fingerprint of its parser's code, so editing one parser re-parses only its
   pages.
2. **crosswalk** (`crosswalk.py`): the registry computed over the parse cache,
   one id per player (FF's `ff_id`) with the app's and FF's slugs attached.
3. **board** (`board.py`, `decision.py`, `approach.py`, `eleven.py`, `worth.py`): `reports/decisions.json`: your cash and what you
   can bid, your squad and its eleven, the line that lists your players for sale, and each standing offer on them
   ranked by what taking it costs the eleven. `approach.approach` says how to get a buy: the bid, the sales that pay for it, and what they cost the eleven.
4. **publish** (`publish.py`): the report to the site, and its ping.
5. **grade** (`grading.py`: each past lock's forecast, kept as `grade_cell`; `python src/grading.py accuracy`
   reads it, star group first), **health** (`health.py`). A failure from here on is noted and the run goes
   on; before it, the run stops.

One copy of each fact: what is fetched is ingest's `src_*` tables. They are read once (`ffcore/loader.py`,
`ffcore/observations.py`) and swept once (`ffcore/state.py`): every derived fact (the forecast, prices, the ledger,
the auction's looks, the decision's inputs) is a Python selector over the World as of a cut, computed by that one
sweep and stored nowhere; the live run is a sweep of one cut. A run records what it decided and what it read by
reference (`runs`, `run_measures`, `dec_opportunity`, `grade_cell`, `auction_*`). Replay cuts are the app's
snapshot stamps (`loader.replay_cuts`), so the board, the grade and the backtests are the same sweep. A bid's win
chance is a fixed table (`ffcore/auction_table.py`, regenerated by `tools/auction_table.py`).

## The model

Every forecast is the same product, for every player and jornada
(`ffcore/forecast_facts.py` selects each factor):

    expected points = points per match x fixture x chance he is picked if fit x chance he is fit

- **Points per match** (`forecast_facts.forecast_rate`): last season
  pulled toward his position's typical rate (for a promoted club, that rate
  discounted), then this season pulled toward that. Every "pulled toward"
  in the model is `stats.shrink`.
- **Fixture** (`forecast_facts.forecast_now`): the opponent's attack or defence factor.
- **Picked if fit** (`forecast_facts.forecast_now`): for his next
  match, futbolfantasy's listing (calibrated on fit players) pulled toward
  his record of appearances in matchday squads; later, 60% pulled toward
  that record; with no record, his listing while fit. A flagged player's
  listing is about the injury, so it never counts as selection.
- **Fit** (`forecast_facts.fit_of`): 1 unless flagged. Flagged by futbolfantasy, his
  prognosis ("Duda para la jornada 8", "Baja hasta mediados de octubre";
  a bare status means his next match) places each jornada in a row of the
  availability table, fitted from past prognoses: appearances under that
  prognosis over appearances when listed fit. The LaLiga app adds only who
  has left the league (out for good); its injury flags lag futbolfantasy's.
- One selector, `forecast_facts.cell` (points if he plays, the chance he plays, a
  moment's every player and jornada left), feeds the board and every
  backtest; `forecast_facts.forecast_used` is it as used (no chance for a player who
  arrives at a club after its match).
- **Points history** (`season_facts.scored`): each change in a player's points
  total, given to the latest match his club had played.
- **Uncertainty** (`ffcore/forecast.py`): match-level points as the pool
  of real single-match scores, and one persistent per-player error
  (`PERSISTENT_SHARE`, measured by the backtest). A move's expected gain
  and its chance of helping, and the standings if nobody moves, are
  worked out from them (docs/notes/lfg.md, section 5). A
  jornada under way keeps the elevens that fielded it.
- **Prices** (`ffcore/pricing.py`): values keep their trend; the next h
  updates move by a fitted multiple of the last one, h being the longest
  horizon the data can fit (a rise was still carrying there), not the
  lock: a buy pays its premium once and keeps rising after it. A bid's
  chance of winning is read off the 30 past auctions of the players nearest
  in price trend (the fixed table `ffcore/auction_table.py`, fitted over every closed
  auction: what the winner paid, or asking if nobody bought): rising players draw rivals'
  bids, falling ones almost never. A listing is bid on at the level whose chance
  times the surplus under the walk-away price K x W is greatest (`hurdle.bid`; W the
  points' worth in millions at the exchange rate LAMBDA, K = 0.85; no surplus, no bid).
  The game offers for each listed player every night (an offer expires at the next close; a rival's direct offer lasts 48 h), centred on value
  (median 0.99x, 80% within 0.92-1.07x) and fresh each night. A sale gets
  tonight's offer or what waiting is worth, whichever is more
  (`hurdle.later`, read from `decision.exit_of`: each later night's offer, at
  his value then (`price_fold.price_carry`), the last before the lock always); with no offer yet, his value.
- **Assembly** (`ffcore/loader.py`, `ffcore/state.py`): the one place that reads storage;
  the model's selectors read the World, never a table.

## League rules

The game's rules as of 2026-09-30, and whether the model needs them. A rule
is in the code only if the feed does not already say it and it changes
which move wins.

- **Clauses are paid at once, with no approval, and the money goes to the
  owner.** The board never pays one; it does not list the players a rival can take now. The feed records any move between
  two managers alike, clause or accepted offer ("transfer"); every raid
  in it paid exactly the clause the feed showed.
- **A clause is whatever the feed shows** (the help pages' default of
  purchase price +50% does not hold here: the feed's median is 1.00 of value).
  A raid pays exactly that. Clause buys of rivals' players are not used.
- **No protection after a transfer in this league.** The feed's
  `buyoutClauseLockedEndTime` is not one: Luismi Cruz was taken 6 days and
  Yamal 13 days before it. Every clause counts as payable.
- **Clause buys close before the matchday** (24h by default; 24–72h per
  league). Not modelled: ours paid one 9h before kickoff, and the app
  refuses a closed one anyway.
- **Everything freezes at the jornada's first kickoff, for every player; a player who joins after it cannot play that jornada.**
  Each jornada pays 100,000 x the team's points about 04:40 Madrid after its last match.
- **A negative balance at the matchday's start scores 0 points; debt is
  capped at 20% of team value.** Enforced by `rules.bid_limit` and `rules.cash_at_lock` in the board's funding (`approach.py`) and by
  the app (bids). Not modelled for rivals: the standings ignore every
  manager's cash, so a rival below zero at the lock still scores.
- **Raising a clause costs half the raise** (1M pays for +2M). Not modelled:
  the board lists who is at risk, not what protecting them would cost.
- **Blindaje:** one player per matchday is shielded from clauses for 24h
  (48h in premium leagues). Turned on between matchdays, free. Not modelled:
  the feed has `playerTeam.isShielded`, the parser drops it, and it was never
  true in 302 sampled rows.

Sources: LaLiga Fantasy help ([clauses](https://laligafantasy.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025683593-How-are-release-clauses-set),
[rulebook](https://laligafantasy.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025679853-Rulebook),
[negative balance](https://laligafantasy.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007533594-Can-I-keep-a-negative-balance)),
[blindaje](https://www.guiafantasy.com/noticias/1686/como-funciona-el-blindaje-la-nueva-regla-que-revoluciona-laliga-fantasy).

## Checking it
    python src/grading.py accuracy                # the forecast at each past lock against what was scored: the star group (e >= 4) and all players
    tools/gate --changed | --full                 # ruff, frontiers, mypy and the self-tests (changed modules only, or all); run bare: it takes the lock and cap
    python tools/frontiers.py                     # the layer check: crossings 0
    python tools/golden.py --selftest             # decisions.json and key features at pinned cuts, against tools/golden/
    python tools/golden.py --live [check|bless]   # the same at the 5 live cuts, over the parse cache
    python tools/auction_table.py                 # regenerates ffcore/auction_table.py; bless the goldens with it
    tools/lfg-rules                               # the sale rules over every replay cut, on a scratch copy (~3 min)
    tools/lfg-rules holds | decided               # the hold back-test (#145) over a grid of policies | over the decision's own hold bids, sold by the momentum exit and by the sale rule (~1 min; 4 rows x 40 seeds)
    python tools/cases.py report                  # seven real players end to end (tools/cases/league.json.xz, a
                                                  # slice of the league as ingest stored it): expected vs actual by stage
    tools/lfg-dry-run [stage ... | --compare CUT...]  # the full run against a scratch copy, or a pinned before/after vs main; nothing published
    uv run mypy src tools                         # strict: every function annotated, no bare
                                                  # dict/list; gates selftests (pyproject [tool.mypy])
    python tools/peek.py params                   # the latest run's rows of a kind in the store (peek.py -i: a REPL)

Run them with `PYTHONPATH=src FF_ROOT=<a copy of data>`; rehearse boards on a
copy of `data/`, never the live one. A change goes in when the backtest
says so, judged by squared error (the simulation needs means, and absolute
error rewards forecasts biased low on skewed points). A change that should
not move the board (a refactor) must leave the golden files unchanged; a change that moves a decision or a feature
is `golden.py bless` in the same commit, with the reason.
New code asks the World's selectors for what it needs
rather than reaching through them; ruff flags reads of private members.

## Layout

    src/            run, ingest, sources, crosswalk, decision (the
                    inputs and the opportunities), board, approach, eleven, valuation, worth,
                    publish, grading, salegrade, act, health, stats
    src/ffcore/     parsed (the parse cache), store and records (the record store),
                    tidy (paths, rows with their numbers parsed), clock (now, t, stamps),
                    observations (the rows, read once), state (the World and
                    its sweep), loader (storage to a Loaded, replay_cuts),
                    current, season_facts, ledger, auction_facts, price_fold,
                    forecast_facts, decision_facts (the derived facts, one
                    module per group), auction, auction_table (the fixed win
                    chance), jornadas (locks), forecast and pricing (constants),
                    rules (positions, formations, the debt), names (Name,
                    PlayerKey, AppId), source, futbolfantasy, footballdata,
                    laliga_api, parse, text, auth, league
    tools/          gate, selftests.sh, frontiers.py, golden.py (and golden/),
                    auction_table.py, lfg-rules, lfg-dry-run, capped, locked-run, peek.py,
                    cases.py (and cases/)
    inputs/         league.ini
    data/raw/       archived pages, append-only
    data/tidy/      parse caches (what parse fills is rebuilt from raw: the
                    registry each run; runs, their decisions,
                    grades are records, kept)
    reports/        decisions.json, what the report page draws
